How to join

Two ways in — both invite-only

Scout is a closed, invite-only service. You join by knowing someone already on it. Messaging consent is explicit and opt-in by design.

👋

Added by a friend

An existing Scout user adds you directly by texting their Scout number with your name and phone number. You receive one introductory message from Scout explaining the service and how to opt out. By replying, you confirm enrollment.

Scout: "Hey [Name], [Friend] added you to Scout — tell me somewhere you love and I'll remember it. Reply STOP to opt out anytime."
🔑

Use a personal invite code

Every Scout user has a personal code (like JOSH or GIA). If someone shares their code with you, text it to the Scout number. You're connected instantly and asked for your name. Initiating contact is your opt-in.

You text: JOSH
Scout: "Hey — I'm Scout. What's your first name?"
Scout's SMS number: 616-313-5900
Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Scout sends only conversational recommendation messages — no marketing blasts, no promotions, no third-party ads. Reply STOP at any time to permanently opt out.
Messaging guidelines

How Scout messages you

Message types

Scout sends only direct responses to your messages — recommendation replies, confirmations, and the occasional one-time re-engagement if you've gone quiet. No bulk messages. No marketing. No third-party promotions.

Opt-out anytime

Reply STOP to immediately and permanently unsubscribe. You will receive no further messages. Reply START to re-subscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. These commands always work.

Your data

Your phone number is your identity in Scout. Your recommendations are private — visible only to you and the connections you've opted into. We never sell or share your data. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
