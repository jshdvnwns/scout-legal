Scout

Your personal recommendation memory — by SMS.

Scout is a private, invitation-only service. Text Scout to save your favorite restaurants, bars, coffee shops, contractors, and more. Ask anytime and Scout tells you what's worth it — from your own memory and the people you trust.

No app. No account. Just text.

How it works

Users join Scout by personal invitation only. An existing member shares the Scout phone number directly. Once you have the number, text it to get started.

How opt-in works

Invite flow: An existing Scout user adds you by name and phone number via SMS. You receive one introductory message explaining the service and how to opt out. You opt in by replying to that message.

Direct flow: You receive the Scout number from a friend via personal communication and text it yourself. Your first message is your opt-in.

In both cases, your first message from Scout includes instructions to reply STOP to opt out at any time. Standard message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. You will receive no further messages. Reply START to re-subscribe.
